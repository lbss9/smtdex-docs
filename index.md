# SMTDex

**SMTDex** is a Discord bot where your server's chat becomes a hunting ground for
demons and personas from the *Shin Megami Tensei* and *Persona* series. Talk in
your channels, and wild demons appear — type their name to capture them. Then
level them up, teach them skills, fuse them into stronger forms, trade with other
players, and battle turn-by-turn.

## What you can do

- **Capture** demons that spawn as your community chats.
- **Collect** across the whole compendium — the same demon from different games
  is its own catch, with its own artwork and skillset.
- **Earn Macca and XP** just by being active. Your demons level up as you talk.
- **Learn passives and skills** from the shop to grow faster and hit harder.
- **Fuse** two demons — even across different games — into a new one.
- **Trade** demons and Macca with other players, safely.
- **Battle** by turns, exploiting elemental weaknesses for extra turns.
