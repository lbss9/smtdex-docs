# Passives

Passives are special skills that **boost how much XP and Macca you earn** from
activity. If any demon in your collection knows a passive, you earn more — the
**best** passive you own applies (they don't stack).

## The Fortuna line

| Passive | Bonus to XP & Macca | Cost (Macca) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fortuna I | +5% | 2,000 |
| Fortuna II | +20% | 8,000 |
| Fortuna III | +60% | 25,000 |
| Fortuna IV | +100% | 60,000 |
| Fortuna V | +150% | 150,000 |
| Fortuna VI | +200% | 400,000 |

## How to get one

Teach a passive to any demon through the skill shop:

```
/skill learn demon: a1b2c3 skill: Fortuna III
```

Once learned, the bonus applies immediately to your activity earnings. You can
check your active bonus at any time with `/balance`.

## Tips

- You only need **one** demon with the highest passive you can afford — the best
  one in your collection is what counts.
- Because the bonus applies to **both** XP and Macca, an early Fortuna pays for
  itself over time.
