# Leveling & XP

## Earning XP by being active

You don't grind — you **participate**. While you chat, all of your demons gain
XP together. To keep things fair and light on the servers, XP is granted on an
**activity window**: roughly once per minute of talking, not once per message.
Spamming 500 messages in a minute does **not** give 500× the XP.

## The level curve

A demon's level is derived from its total XP:

- Total XP to reach level *L* = `50 × (L − 1)²`
- Demons cap at **level 99**.

Higher-level demons need progressively more XP for the next level, so early
levels come quickly and late levels take dedication.

## Other ways to gain XP

- **Winning [battles](battles.html)** grants XP to the demon that fought.
- **[Passives](passives.html)** like *Fortuna* multiply the XP you earn from
  activity.

## Checking progress

`/collection` shows each demon's current level. `/balance` shows your highest
level and your active passive bonus.
