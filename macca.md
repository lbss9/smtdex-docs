# Macca & Economy

**Macca** is the in-game currency. You spend it on skills and passives, and you
can include it in trades.

## Earning Macca

Like XP, Macca is earned on an **activity window** — you gain a set amount for
being active in chat, at most once per minute. Being present and chatting is what
grows your wallet; spamming does not.

## Checking your balance

```
/balance
```

Shows your Macca, how many demons you own, your highest-level demon, and your
current **passive bonus** to XP and Macca.

## Spending Macca

- **[Skills & the shop](skills.html)** — teach your demons new skills.
- **[Passives](passives.html)** — permanent multipliers to your earning.

## Trading Macca

Macca can be offered in a trade alongside (or instead of) demons. See
**[Trading](trading.html)**. Transfers are atomic: either the whole trade goes
through or nothing does.
