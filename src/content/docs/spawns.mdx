Spawns every **3 minutes** (configurable, min 30 s) and last **3 minutes**. **1** active spawn per channel. If nobody finishes, the embed becomes a flee (no name).

## Capture or battle

**44% battle / 56% capture**.

**Capture:** type the **name** (accents and case ignored). Needs a slot (**&lt; 100**). Reward: demon + capture macca + **28%** drops (unique **18%**) + **+2 Renown**.

**Battle:** only **1** player clicks **Battle**. Boss + **3 adds**. A win captures **the boss only**. Adds vanish. Fight XP + fight macca + capture macca + **42%** drops + **+5 Renown**.

## Moon and stars

Every instance rolls 1–5★. Peak/high (near new/full) and **weekend (Brasília)** push 4★ and 5★. `/moon` and `/moon demon:Name`.

The moon does **not** change exploration or the Hall.

## What never spawns

**DLC:** Artemis, Cleopatra, Mephisto, Konohana Sakuya, Dagda, Demi-fiend, Demi-fiend A, Nuwa, Nuwa A.  
**Mitamas.** **3+ parent** specials.

Elements **do** spawn — Jade farm. See [where to farm](../farm/).
