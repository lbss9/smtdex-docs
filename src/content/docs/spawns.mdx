title: Spawns
description: How wild demons appear, how long they last, and how the moon changes the odds.
---

Wild demons appear in the server about every **three minutes**. Each spawn lasts **three minutes**. If nobody claims or finishes the fight in time, it disappears.

## Capture or battle

Each spawn is one of two kinds, with equal chance:

- **Capture** — type the demon’s name in chat to catch it.
- **Battle** — fight the wild encounter. Winning can capture the demon.

## Stars

Every instance rolls **1 to 5 stars**. Higher stars are rarer and stronger. New moon, full moon, and weekends (Brasília time) push the roll toward 4★ and 5★.

## Moon

`/moon` shows the current phase and the spawn chance of each species. You can also look up one demon.

New moon and full moon are the strongest windows. First and last quarter give a milder bump.

## Demons that never spawn

These species only come from **fusion**. They will not appear in the wild:

Artemis, Cleopatra, Mephisto, Konohana Sakuya, Dagda, Demi-fiend, Demi-fiend A, Nuwa, Nuwa A.
