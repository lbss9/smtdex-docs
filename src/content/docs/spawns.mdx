Wild demons appear about every **three minutes** and last **three minutes**. If nobody captures or finishes the fight, the message becomes a flee embed (image + timestamps, no name).

## Capture or battle

Even split: **capture** (type the name) or **battle** (fight the encounter).

## Stars and moon

Every instance rolls 1–5 stars. New moon, full moon, and weekends (Brasília time) push 4★ and 5★. `/moon` shows the phase and odds.

## What never spawns

**DLC** (Artemis, Cleopatra, Mephisto, Konohana Sakuya, Dagda, Demi-fiend, Demi-fiend A, Nuwa, Nuwa A) and the four **Mitamas**. Elements (Erthys, Aeros, Aquans, Flaemis) **do spawn** — that is how you farm Jade and color gems.
