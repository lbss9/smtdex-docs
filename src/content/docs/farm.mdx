## Macca

| Source | Amount | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arena / spawn / Hall win | Sum of `enemy_level × 6` | `/battle` |
| Exploration | Same formula × **28% / 40% / 55%** (Surface / Penumbra / Abyss); boss **×1.35** | `/battle` → PvE → Exploration |
| Name capture | `floor((12 + lv×2 + ★×12) × 0.8)` | Type the name on a capture spawn |
| Chat | **+10** | One message every **60 s** |
| Expedition success | 800 / 1,600 / 3,200 / 6,400 / 12,000 | Kalpa I–V |
| Sell | `sell × quantity` | `/shop` → Sell |

Capture lv 2 ★1 ≈ **22**. Capture lv 40 ★3 ≈ **102**. One lv 50 Arena enemy = **350**.

## XP

Curve: `50 × (level − 1)²` up to **99**. One battle grants at most **+8** levels.

Per enemy, **each** party member gets `enemy_level × 8` with a `1.15` level-gap multiplier (clamped −10…+10) and catch-up if the enemy is 11+ levels higher (up to ×3.2).

| Source | XP |
| --- | --- |
| Arena / spawn / Hall | Full formula |
| Exploration | Formula × 28% / 40% / 55% |
| Chat | **+20** on **every** demon (60 s) |
| Expedition success | 2,200 / 3,600 / 5,800 / 9,200 / 14,000 **per demon** |

## Gems (Jade, Ruby, Amethyst, Opal, Sapphire)

Unique drop **18%** when the enemy matches the name or race.

| Gem | From | Sell | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Jade** | Any **Element** | 250 | Every Rag's buy |
| **Ruby** | **Flaemis** and Dragon race | 400 | Ara Mitama |
| **Amethyst** | **Aeros** | 400 | Nigi Mitama |
| **Opal** | **Erthys** | 400 | Kusi Mitama |
| **Sapphire** | **Aquans**, Snake, Aqua | 400 | Saki Mitama |

Main farm: **Suginami Tunnels** (open from the start, 5 rooms, all 4 Elements). Surface already drops. Elements also spawn in the wild. **Mitamas never spawn.**

Do not sell gems if you still need Mitamas — 250–400 macca loses to infusion.

## Elements and Mitamas

Three paths:

1. **Fuse 2** — two **different** Elements (dyad ~92%). Pairs: Aeros+Flaemis / Aquans+Erthys → Ara; Aeros+Erthys → Nigi; Aeros+Aquans / Erthys+Flaemis → Kusi; Aquans+Flaemis → Saki.
2. **Rag's** (`/rags`) — Element 1 Jade or 5,000; Mitama 1 Jade + 1 color or 10,000; Premium 3 Jade + 2 color or 20,000.
3. **Chamber** (`/fuse`) — pick the demon, type, and quantity. **100%**. Shows **STR 10 → 16**. +20% on two stats up to **999**. HP/MP do not rise.

Mitamas **cannot** join party, battle, Fuse 2/3/4, or Reverse.

## Exploration vs Expedition vs Arena

- **Suginami Surface** — early gems, 5 short rooms.
- **Abyss** on later tunnels — better Skill Cards and more loot per room.
- **Expedition** — send the whole roster on a timer (30 min–8 h). Need **power ≥ 100%** of the Kalpa or you return empty. Locks party, fusion, and chat XP.
- **Arena** — full XP/macca, one fight, +Renown.
- **Spawn Battle** — capture the boss + loot + more Renown than Arena.

Tunnel and Kalpa details: [Exploration](../exploration/), [Expedition](../expedition/).
