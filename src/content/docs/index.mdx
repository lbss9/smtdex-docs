SMTDex turns your server into a hunting ground for demons from *Shin Megami Tensei V* and *Nocturne*. Talk in the channels, wild demons appear, and you capture them by typing their name. Then you build a party, fuse stronger forms — including **Mitama** — and battle.

<CardGrid stagger>
  <Card title="Hunt" icon="magnifier">
    Spawns about every three minutes. Type the name to capture, or fight. Elements drop Jade and color gems.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Collect" icon="star">
    Each instance has 1–5 stars. Attributes cap at 999; HP and MP at 9,999. Use `/inventory` and `/info`.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Battle" icon="rocket">
    `/battle`: Arena, Exploration, Expedition, Infernal Hall, Duelo, or Confisco.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Fuse" icon="puzzle">
    `/fuse` covers the chart, specials, and Mitama Chamber. Rag's Jewelry is in `/shop`.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

SMTDex is an unofficial fan project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by ATLUS or SEGA.
