SMTDex turns your server into a hunting ground for demons from *Shin Megami Tensei V*. Talk in the channels, wild demons appear, and you capture them by typing their name. Then you build a party, fuse stronger forms, and battle.

<CardGrid stagger>
  <Card title="Hunt" icon="magnifier">
    Spawns appear about every three minutes. Type the demon’s name to capture it, or fight it in a wild encounter.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Collect" icon="star">
    Each instance has 1–5 stars. Check `/compendium` for your demons and purchased skills, and `/info` for catalog details.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Battle" icon="rocket">
    `/battle` opens a menu: **Arena** (PvE), **Duelo** (PvP with parties), or **Confisco** (PvP for a single demon).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Fuse" icon="puzzle">
    `/fuse` supports 2-, 3-, and 4-parent recipes plus reverse fusion. The result’s stars are the minimum of the parents.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
