<Aside type="tip" title="New in 3.0.0 — the Nocturne rebalance">
**7-star tiers** (1★ common → 7★ 🌈 Mythic), tier-based stat caps, IVs removed, flat **+1 Mitama** infusions, and a Nocturne-style damage formula. The old `/inventory` and `/party` are gone — everything now lives inside [`/player`](commands/#player). See [What's new in 3.0.0](whats-new-300/).
</Aside>

SMTDex turns your server into a hunting ground for demons from *Shin Megami Tensei V* and *Nocturne*. Talk in the channels, wild demons appear, and you capture them by typing their name. Then you build a party, fuse stronger forms — including **Mitama** — and battle.
