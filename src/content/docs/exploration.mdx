`/battle` → PvE → **Exploration**. A chain of rooms to a boss. The moon does **not** change tunnels. Enemies do **not** scale to your party — they use catalog level and stars.

Each room is an isolated fight: HP/MP/ailments reset. Magatsuhi and the bag persist for the run.

## Tunnels

Clear **Surface + Penumbra + Abyss** of the previous tunnel to unlock the next: Suginami (5) → Nakano (20) → Shinjuku (8) → Babel (10) → Ichigaya (8) → Shibuya (10) → Shinagawa (12) → Ueno (12) → Mirages (15).

Suginami is the **gem farm**: Erthys, Aeros, Aquans, and Flaemis are in the pool. See [Items & gems](../items/).

## Depth

| | Mobs | Yield vs Arena |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Surface** | tunnel pool | 28% (boss ×1.35) |
| **Penumbra** | more enemies | 40% (boss ×1.35) |
| **Abyss** | even more; 8% rare boss | 55% (boss ×1.35) |

The last room is the boss (up to 10 enemies vs a party of 4).
