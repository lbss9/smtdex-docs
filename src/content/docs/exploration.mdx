`/battle` → PvE → **Exploration** → tunnel → depth → party → manual/auto.

A room sequence up to the boss. The moon does **not** change tunnels. Enemies do **not** equalize — they enter at catalog level and ★. Each room is isolated: HP/MP/ailments reset. Magatsuhi and the bag persist. Loss/flee ends the run; loot from earlier rooms **stays**.

Gem farm: [where to farm](../farm/).

## Depth

Enemies per room (5-room tunnel): Surface 2→3→4→5→**boss 4** · Penumbra 3→4→6→7→**6** · Abyss 4→6→8→10→**8**. Longer tunnels interpolate. Cap **10** enemies. Abyss: **8%** rare boss.

| | Yield vs Arena | Item/room | Item rank | Boss card | Card rank |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | --- | ---: |
| **Surface** | 28% (boss ×1.35) | 18% | 2 | 22% / slot | 8 |
| **Penumbra** | 40% (boss ×1.35) | 28% | 3 | 32% / slot | 16 |
| **Abyss** | 55% (boss ×1.35) | 40% | 5 | 42% / slot | 28 |

Boss: up to **4** Skill Card slots. Magatsuhi **never** drops.
