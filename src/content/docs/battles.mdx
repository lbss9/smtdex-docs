A party holds up to **four** demons. Edit with `/party`. Combat attributes (STR, MAG, VIT, AGI, LCK) cap at **999**. HP and MP cap at **9,999**. Per-hit damage caps at 99,999.

## `/battle`

Private menu: **PvE** or **PvP**.

PvE: **Arena**, **Exploration**, **Expedition**, **Infernal Hall**.  
PvP: **Duelo** (parties) or **Confisco** (one demon; the loser loses it).

Use **Fechar**. Idle menus close after a minute. Confisco needs a double confirm (90 s).

## Arena

Player vs environment. After picking a party you can play turns or auto. Wins grant XP and macca. Wild fight spawns work the same. Arena **scales** level/stars to the party.

## Duelo

PvP with parties. Nobody loses demons. No XP or macca.

## Confisco

PvP with **one demon each**. The loser **loses that demon**. Both players confirm. This is the only confiscation mode.

## Magatsuhi

Party gauge 0–100. Magatsuhi skills (rank 50) appear in the skill select when full and drain the whole bar without spending a Press Turn. See [Magatsuhi](../magatsuhi/).
