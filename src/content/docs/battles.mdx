A party has up to **four** demons. Create and edit parties with `/party`. Arena and Duelo use a party; Confisco uses a single demon.

## `/battle`

There are no subcommands and no typed opponent. The command opens a private menu:

1. Choose **PvE** or **PvP**.
2. PvE goes straight to **Arena**. PvP asks for **Duelo** or **Confisco**.
3. In PvP, pick the opponent with Discord’s user selector — you never type a name.
4. Arena and Duelo: pick a party. Confisco: pick **one demon**.
5. The opponent must accept. In Confisco, both players also confirm the stake.

Use **Fechar** to abort setup. Idle setup menus close after one minute. The Confisco dual confirmation lasts 90 seconds.

## Arena

Player versus the environment. After you pick a party, you choose whether to control turns or let the fight run automatically. Winning grants XP and macca. Wild spawn battles work the same way when the spawn is a fight.

## Duelo

Player versus player with parties. The winner is decided in combat. You do not lose demons.

## Confisco

Player versus player with **one demon each**. The loser **loses that demon** — it is transferred to the winner. Both players must confirm before the fight starts. This is the only mode that confiscates a demon.

The word *sacrifice* is not used in the bot. The mode is called **Confisco**.
