Since **3.1.0** you can do two new things with duplicates:

1. **Convert to Essence** — turns the demon into a stackable item that doesn't take a demon slot.
2. **Evolve** a demon one tier up (1★ → 2★ → ... → 7★ 🌈 Mythic).

<Aside type="tip">
Roster full? Convert dupes to Essence before catching more. Each Essence stacks up to **99 per species** in **Valuables** — outside the 100-demon cap.
</Aside>

## Essence — what it is

Every species has its own Essence: **Essence of Jack Frost**, **Essence of Pixie**, etc. They live in `/player → Items → Valuables` and have two uses:

- **Star evolution** cost.
- **Bank** for rare species (store without eating a slot).

**How to get it:**

- `/player → Demons → pick a dupe → 🔄 Essence` — irreversible; the demon vanishes and becomes 1× Essence.
- **Confiscation** (PvP): winning nets the demon **and** 1× Essence of its species.
- Auto-convert (coming soon): flip `autoConvertDupes` in your profile and every duplicate capture goes straight to Essence.

**Rules:**

- Only duplicates convert. If you own only 1 of the species, the button doesn't show.
- Favorites and demons in party/expedition **won't** convert.

## Star evolution

`/player → Demons → pick the target → 🔼 Evolve`.

### Cost per tier

| Up | Essence | Jade | Mitama (Essence) | Macca |
| :---: | :--- | ---: | :--- | ---: |
| 1★ → 2★ | 3× any | — | — | 5,000 |
| 2★ → 3★ | 3× any | 1× | — | 15,000 |
| 3★ → 4★ | 3× any | 2× | — | 40,000 |
| 4★ → 5★ | 3× any | 3× | — | 100,000 |
| **5★ → 6★** 💠 | **3× SAME species** | 5× | 1× (any Mitama) | 300,000 |
| **6★ → 7★** 🌈 | **3× SAME species** | 10× | 1× Ara + 1× Nigi + 1× Kusi + 1× Saki | 800,000 |

**"Any"** — 1★→5★ upgrades accept any species/tier Essence.  
**"SAME species"** — starting at 5★→6★, the Essence must match the demon being evolved. That's where the farm kicks in.

### What changes on the demon

- **Tier** goes up by 1 (multipliers table on [stars & evolution](../stars-evolution/) applies).
- **Level cap** and **stat cap** expand instantly.
- **Level, moveset, Mitama bonuses, learned Skill Cards** all preserved.
- **XP** stays too — you can keep grinding where you were.

### What must line up

- Target can't be at 7★ (top).
- Resources checked live before confirming — the button shows ✅/❌ per line.
- All inside a transaction: if anything fails, **nothing** is debited.

## Tips

- **The 5→6 wall**: up to 5★ you can dip into any dupes lying around. From there you need **the same demon three times at 5★** — it becomes a project.
- **Bank Mitamas as Essence.** Ara / Nigi / Kusi / Saki each convert to Essence the same way. They only matter at the 6★→7★ gate, so stockpiling early pays off.
- **Don't convert your only copy.** The button blocks by mistake, but if you own 2 Pixies and one is favorited, the other is effectively your only — and favorites don't convert either.
- **PvP Confiscation is stronger** — you get the demon AND 1× Essence toward your own evolutions.
