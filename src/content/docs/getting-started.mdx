## Pick a starter

Use `/start` and choose Pixie, Slime, Onmoraki, Cait Sith, or Angel. You get that demon and **500 macca**. Once only.

## What to do next

1. Talk in the channels. Spawns appear about every **three minutes**.
2. Type the **exact name** to capture, or fight the encounter.
3. Open `/inventory` for demons, items, and Skill Cards.
4. Build a party with `/party` (up to four; Mitamas cannot join).
5. Fight with `/battle` — PvE (Arena, Exploration, Expedition, Infernal Hall) or PvP.

Private menus close after **one idle minute**.

## Commands you will use a lot

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/moon` | Moon phase and spawn odds |
| `/info` | Catalog or an owned demon (attributes cap 999, HP/MP 9,999, Mitama included) |
| `/fuse` | Fusion, Mitama Chamber, and Rag's Jewelry |
| `/mail` | Send and receive demons, items, and macca |
| `/shop` | Item shop; sell a category or everything |
| `/balance` | Macca, collection size, highest level |
