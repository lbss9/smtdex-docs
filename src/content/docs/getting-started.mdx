## 1. Begin

`/start` → Pixie, Slime, Onmoraki, Cait Sith, or Angel + **500 macca**. Once.

## 2. Hunt

Talk in the channel. Spawns every **3 minutes**, last **3 minutes**. **56%** capture (type the **name**) · **44%** fight (**Battle** button). You need roster room (**100** demons).

`/moon` shows the phase and odds. DLC and Mitamas **do not** spawn. Elements **do**.

## 3. Organize

| Command | For |
| --- | --- |
| `/player` | One menu: **Items**, **Demons**, **Parties**. Replaced `/inventory` and `/party`. |
| `/info` | Catalog species or one of yours. HP/MP up to **9,999**; STR/MAG/VIT/AGI/LUCK use **per-tier caps** (50–75). |
| `/profile` / `/balance` | Macca, MAG, Renown, W/L, public Ref. |

Star system explained: [stars & evolution](../stars-evolution/).

## 4. Fight

`/battle` → PvE (Arena, Exploration, Expedition, Hall) or PvP (Duel, Confiscation). Manual or auto.

First farm: **Exploration → Suginami Tunnels → Surface** for Jade and gems. Guide: [where to farm](../farm/).

## 5. Fuse and Mitama

`/fuse` — Fuse 2/3/4, Reverse, Chamber. Two different Elements become a Mitama. Infuse in the Chamber: **+1 flat** on two stats per infusion, cap **+20 total per stat**. Rag's is `/rags`.

## 6. Magatsuhi

Fill the gauge in combat. Buy the card at Rag's (Magatsuhi tab, **Jade**). Teach any demon. Use it at **100**.

Private menus close after **1 idle minute**.
