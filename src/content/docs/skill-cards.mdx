---
title: Skill Cards
description: Which skills drop as cards, where to farm them, and which stay on the owner.
---

Every **transferable** skill — active and passive — exists as a Skill Card. **Signature** skills stay on their demon: no card, no fusion inheritance.

Full table: [skill catalog](../catalog-skills/) (Card / Source columns).

## Drop chance

| Source | Chance |
| --- | --- |
| Arena / spawn victory | **50%** for 1 card. **100%** on a **full moon** |
| Exploration boss | Up to 4 slots (rank cap 8 / 16 / 28) |
| Expedition | 8–30 cards (Kalpa I–V rank caps 8 / 14 / 22 / 32 / 40) |
| `/rags` Magatsuhi | Every **active** Magatsuhi, Jade only |
| System mail | Any catalog card |

Signatures never enter those pools.

## What you cannot get as a card

Hall signatures at rank 99, plus Demi-fiend's Gaea Rage, Deadly Fury, and Chaotic Will. Fuse or capture the owner (Demi-fiend is DLC — hall only).

Commons in a hall kit (Charge, Dekunda, Megidolaon, Mediarahan, Enduring Soul…) **are** cards.

## Fusion

Pool up to 25. Pick up to 8. **Only signatures** stay out. Racial innates, passives, and Magatsuhi **inherit**.

Learn in `/inventory` → Skill Cards. The card is consumed.
