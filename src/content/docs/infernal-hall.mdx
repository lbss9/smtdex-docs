`/battle` → PvE → **Infernal Hall** → Fiend → party → manual/auto.

**1 Fiend vs the whole party** (max 4). The next floor unlocks after you kill the previous. Floors are refarmable.

Order: Matador → Daisoujou → Hell Biker → White Rider → Red Rider → Black Rider → Pale Rider → Mother Harlot → Trumpeter.

## Equalize

Level ≥ party average. Stats ≥ average (cap 999). HP = max(built, **2.8× party HP**), cap **9,999**. Damage × `(1 + 0.25 × (allies − 1))` — a party of 4 → **×1.75**. **MP is unlimited** — the Fiend never runs dry.
