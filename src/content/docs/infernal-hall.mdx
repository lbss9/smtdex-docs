`/battle` → PvE → **Infernal Hall** → Boss → party → manual/auto.

**1 Boss vs the whole party** (max 4). The next floor unlocks after you clear the previous. Floors are refarmable.

Order (21 floors): 9 Fiends → 4 Qadishtu → Beelzebub → Metatron → Aradia → Adam → Kagutsuchi → Demi-fiend → Satan → **Lucifer**.

## Equalize

Level ≥ party average. Hall bosses **bypass the per-tier stat cap** and use inflated statlines. HP = max(built, **2.8× party HP**), cap **9,999**. Damage × `(1 + 0.25 × (allies − 1))` — a party of 4 → **×1.75**. **MP is unlimited** — the boss never runs dry.
