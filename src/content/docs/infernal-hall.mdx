`/battle` → PvE → **Infernal Hall**. Nine Fiends in order, **one versus your party**.

Matador → Daisoujou → Hell Biker → White Rider → Red Rider → Black Rider → Pale Rider → Mother Harlot → Trumpeter.

The next floor unlocks only after you kill the previous one. Cleared floors can be farmed again.

Fiend HP = **2.8 × total party HP** (cap 999). Fiend damage scales with ally count. Each win drops that Fiend’s **Candelabrum** (Valuables; not in the shop or generic drops).

Nocturne mechanics: Red Capote, Soul Divide, Pestilence, Hell Exhaust, Evil Melody / God's Bow, Holy Melody.
