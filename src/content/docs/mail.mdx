---
title: Mail
description: Send demons and macca, claim everything at once, or send a package back.
---

`/mail` opens your inbox. Pass a user (and optional macca or message) to start a new package.

You can attach demons you own that are not favorites and not already held in another mail. The body can be up to 500 characters.

## Inbox

Pending mail lists each attached demon by name and level — not a generic count.

From the inbox you can:

- Open a mail and **receive** it (claim demons and macca).
- **Return** it to the sender (with confirmation).
- **Receber todos** — claim every pending mail in one tap. The embed reports `Recebidos: N · Falharam: M` if anything fails (for example a demon that can no longer be delivered).

The sender can cancel a pending mail from the sent folder. Demons in transit are held until the mail is claimed, returned, or cancelled.

There is no player-to-player trade command. Mail is how you send things.
