---
title: Mail
description: Send demons and macca, charge a PayIn price, or send a package back.
---

`/mail` opens your inbox. Pass a user (and optional macca, PayIn price, or message) to start a new package.

You can attach demons you own that are not favorites and not already held in another mail. The body can be up to 500 characters.

## PayIn

Set `preco` when composing to charge the recipient. The package (demons and/or gifted macca) stays held until they **pay that price** and receive it. The macca they pay goes to the sender.

If they cannot afford it, the mail stays pending. They can still **return** it without paying. **Receber todos** only claims free mail — PayIn packages must be accepted one by one.

## Inbox

Pending mail lists each attached demon by name and level — not a generic count.

From the inbox you can:

- Open a mail and **receive** it (or **Pagar X e receber** when there is a PayIn).
- **Return** it to the sender (with confirmation).
- **Receber todos** — claim every free pending mail in one tap. The embed reports `Recebidos: N · Falharam: M` if anything fails.

The sender can cancel a pending mail from the sent folder. Demons in transit are held until the mail is claimed, returned, or cancelled.

**System** mail (dashboard) can attach catalog items and Skill Cards, including Magatsuhi. Returning System mail **discards** the attachments.

There is no player-to-player trade command. Mail is how you send things.
