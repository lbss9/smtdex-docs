**284** species in the catalog. Each demon you own is an instance: level, stars, IVs, moveset, nickname, and Mitama bonus.

Full list: [demon catalog](../catalog-demons/). Skills: [skill catalog](../catalog-skills/).

## Stars

1★ to 5★ (💠 legendary). They multiply stats/HP/MP: **1.0 / 1.1 / 1.25 / 1.45 / 1.75**. Fusion inherits the **minimum of the parents**. Mitama infusion does **not** change stars.

## Stats and IVs

HP/MP cap **9,999**. STR/MAG/VIT/AGI/LCK cap **999**. Damage per hit **99,999**.

IVs **0–31** per stat (HP, STR, MAG, VIT, AGI, LCK; max total 186). Fusion rolls fresh IVs.

**Potentials** (−9 to +9) change species damage, healing, ailment, and MP. They do **not** apply to Magatsuhi.

Mitama: +20% on two stats up to **999**. Mitamas use **☯** and cannot join a party. See [Fusion](../fusion/).

## Resistances and affinities

14 affinities: physical + 9 elements + poison / nerve / panic / charm. Values: weak, neutral, resist, null, repel, drain. Numeric affinities in the catalog push skill power.

## Inventory

Cap **100** (pending mail does not count). `/inventory` lists, details, deletes. An expedition locks delete and learn. `/info` looks up the species or the instance.

## What never spawns

DLC (Artemis, Cleopatra, Mephisto, Konohana Sakuya, Dagda, Demi-fiend, Demi-fiend A, Nuwa, Nuwa A), Mitamas, and **3+ parent** specials. Elements **do** spawn.
