Each owned demon is its own instance: level, stars, moveset, and nickname.

## Stars

| Stars | Label |
| --- | --- |
| 1★ | ⭐ |
| 2★ | ⭐⭐ |
| 3★ | ⭐⭐⭐ |
| 4★ | 🌟🌟🌟🌟 |
| 5★ | 💠 Legendary |

Stars multiply stats, HP, and MP. 1★ is baseline; 5★ is far above average.

On fusion, the result’s stars are the **minimum of the parents**. A 5★ fused with a 2★ becomes 2★. The preview tells you which parent limited the roll.

## Compendium

`/compendium` opens a home screen with two buttons:

- **Demons** — your collection, details, and moveset swaps.
- **Skills** — skills you bought from the shop.

There are no slash subcommands. Pick everything in the UI, then **Fechar** when you are done.

## Info

`/info` looks up a species in the catalog, or one of your owned demons if you point it at an instance. Use it for resistances, skills, and potentials.
