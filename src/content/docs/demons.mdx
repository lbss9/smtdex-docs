**287** species in the catalog. Each demon you own is an instance: level, stars, moveset, nickname, and Mitama bonus.

Full list: [demon catalog](../catalog-demons/). Skills: [skill catalog](../catalog-skills/).

## Stars

**1★ to 7★** — 6★ is 💠 **Legendary**, 7★ is 🌈 **Mythic**. They multiply stats/HP/MP:

| Tier | Multiplier | Level cap | Stat cap |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1★ | ×0.85 | 20 | 50 |
| 2★ | ×1.00 | 30 | 50 |
| 3★ | ×1.15 | 40 | 50 |
| 4★ | ×1.30 | 50 | 50 |
| 5★ | ×1.50 | 60 | 50 |
| 6★ 💠 | ×1.75 | 75 | 60 |
| 7★ 🌈 | ×2.00 | 99 | 75 |

Fusion inherits the **minimum tier of the parents**. Mitama infusion does **not** change stars. Details: [stars & evolution](../stars-evolution/).

## Stats

HP/MP cap **9,999**. STR / MAG / VIT / AGI / LCK use the **per-tier stat cap** above. Damage per hit **99,999**.

**No IVs since 3.0.0** — two demons of the same species **and** tier have identical base stats. Individual difference only comes from **tier** and **infused Mitama**.

**Potentials** (−9 to +9) change species damage, healing, ailment, and MP. They do **not** apply to Magatsuhi.

Mitama: **+1 flat** on two stats per infusion, cap **+20 total per stat**. Mitamas use **☯** and cannot join a party. See [Fusion](../fusion/).

## Resistances and affinities

14 affinities: physical + 9 elements + poison / nerve / panic / charm. Values: weak, neutral, resist, null, repel, drain. Numeric affinities in the catalog push skill power.

## Inventory

Cap **100** (pending mail does not count). `/player` → **Demons** lists, details, deletes. An expedition locks delete and learn. `/info` looks up the species or the instance.

## What never spawns

DLC (Artemis, Cleopatra, Mephisto, Konohana Sakuya, Dagda, Demi-fiend, Demi-fiend A, Nuwa, Nuwa A), Mitamas, and **3+ parent** specials. Elements **do** spawn.
