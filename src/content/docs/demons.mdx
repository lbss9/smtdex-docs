Each owned demon is its own instance: level, stars, moveset, nickname, and **Mitama bonus**.

## Stars

1★ to 5★ (💠 legendary). Stars multiply stats, HP, and MP. Fusion results inherit the **minimum of the parents**. Mitama infusion does **not** change the recipient’s stars.

## Stats

HP and MP cap at **9,999**. Strength, Magic, Vitality, Agility, and Luck cap at **999**. Per-hit damage caps at 99,999.

Mitama infusion adds +20% to two stats until **2× unbuffed base**. Mitamas themselves show **☯** (no stars) and cannot join a party. See [Fusion & Mitama](../fusion/) and [Items & gems](../items/).

## Inventory

`/inventory` lists the collection (cap 100). `/info` looks up a species or an instance you own.
