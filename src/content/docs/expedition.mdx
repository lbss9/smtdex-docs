title: Expedition
description: Amala Labyrinth, five Kalpas, roster power, and loot.

`/battle` → PvE → **Expedition** → destination → confirm.

The **entire free roster** (not mail) enters the Amala Labyrinth. This is not a turn fight: the bot measures power, waits out the clock, and returns the result. Power = sum of level × rarity × IVs of free demons at send time.

| --- | --- |
| ≥ **100%** | Success — full loot |
| **50–99%** | Fail at the end — **no loot** |

**Never drops:** Magatsuhi cards, Soma, Masakado Crest, Shard/Crystal.

## What locks

Fusion, party, PvE/PvP, spawn **Battle**, delete/learn on run demons, mailing demons, chat XP/macca. **Name capture still works**; the new demon does **not** join the run.

`/battle` during a run: *"Your demons are in the Amala Labyrinth."*
