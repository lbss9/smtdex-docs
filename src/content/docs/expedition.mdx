`/battle` → PvE → **Expedition**. Your **entire roster** (favorites included; pending mail excluded) enters the Amala Labyrinth. It is not turn combat: the bot scores roster power, waits out the clock, and posts the result in the channel.

## Kalpas

| Destination | Time | Power | Macca | XP/demon |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kalpa I | 30 min | 720 | 800 | 2,200 |
| Kalpa II | 1 h | 1,800 | 1,600 | 3,600 |
| Kalpa III | 2 h | 4,200 | 3,200 | 5,800 |
| Kalpa IV | 4 h | 9,000 | 6,400 | 9,200 |
| Kalpa V | 8 h | 18,000 | 12,000 | 14,000 |

Power ≥ 100% of the requirement → success (XP, items, Skill Cards, macca). 50–99% → fail, no loot. Below 50% → **collapse** at half the timer.

While a run is going, fusion, party, PvE/PvP, and spawn fights are locked. Name-capture on spawns **still works**.
