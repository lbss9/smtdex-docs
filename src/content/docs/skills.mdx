**802** skills in the datastore. Full list: [skill catalog](../catalog-skills/).

## Types

Physical, magical, heal, buff, debuff, ailment, passive. Elements: physical, fire, ice, lightning, force, earth, water, psico, holy, dark, almighty.

Cost: MP (magic/support) or HP (physical). Magatsuhi shows **MAG** and only appears when the gauge is full.

## Moveset

Max **8** skills per demon. Learn in `/inventory` → Skill Cards. The card is consumed. A full moveset asks for a swap.

## Fusion inheritance

Pool up to 25 (species learnset + parents). You pick up to 8. **Do not inherit:** racial innates and Magatsuhi.

## Magatsuhi

Rank 50. Buy at [`/rags`](../shop/#rags-jewelry--demons) (Jade only). Any demon can learn it. See [Magatsuhi](../magatsuhi/).

## Potential

−9 to +9 per species category. Changes damage, healing, ailment, and MP. Does **not** apply to Magatsuhi.
