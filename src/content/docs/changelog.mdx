Dated record of what shipped. The bot is at **2.7.6**.

## 2.7.6 — 15 August 2026

Balance pass from the 15/08 report. Fight macca **fell to `lv×6`, from `lv×7`**. Fight XP **rose to `lv×9`, from `lv×8`**. Spawns **fell to 44% battle / 56% capture, from 50/50**. ★1 weight **rose**; moon/weekend ★5 **fell**. Hard/Merciless bosses got easier; Safety got less free. Kalpa IV/V power **fell to 7,400 / 14,400, from 9,000 / 18,000**. Shop prices and skill catalog **stayed**. Chamber preview **dropped Quantity** and now marks **2× cap** stats instead of `414 → 414`.

## 2.7.5 — 15 August 2026

Rag's Jewelry **left `/shop`**. **`/rags`** opens Elements, Mitamas, and Magatsuhi. `/shop` is Items and Sell only.

## 2.7.4 — 15 August 2026

The Mitama Chamber preview **now shows current and final combat stats** of the recipient (`STR 24 → 28 (+4)`), instead of only naming which attributes rise.

## 2.7.3 — 15 August 2026

Mitama buys and chamber infusions **rose to 20 per batch**, from 99. Leftovers **return** if the 999 cap hits mid-batch. The shop **dropped the item list from the embed** and the duplicate buttons: tabs are **Items / Sell / Rag's / Close**.

## 2.7.2 — 15 August 2026

**Bosses have unlimited MP**, including Infernal Hall Fiends. Physical skills still cost HP. The boss refills to max MP at the start of each turn.

## 2.7.1 — 15 August 2026

Every skill and item now has **effect text**. Discord skill selects show what the move does. Wiki catalogs gained an **Effect** column (802 skills, 107 items).

## 2.7.0 — 15 August 2026

Rag's Jewelry now sells **every active Magatsuhi** as a Skill Card. **Jade only** (3 / 4 / 6 / 8 / 12 by skill rank). **Any demon** can learn any Magatsuhi in `/inventory`.

## 2.6.1 — 15 August 2026

**Rag's Jewelry** **left `/fuse`, from living next to fusion**. It opens in `/shop` under **Rag's Jewelry**.

## 2.6.0 — 15 August 2026

The **Mitama Chamber** is now the only way to infuse. Mitamas **left Fusion 2 / 3 / 4 and Reverse, from being usable parents**. Pick the demon, type, and quantity.

## 2.5.1 — 15 August 2026

Rag's Jewelry **gained a quantity field, from buying one at a time**. Mitama infusion can inject several of the same type in one confirm (macca × count), stopping at the 2× stat cap.

## 2.5.0 — 15 August 2026

Mitamas **left the party, from being usable fighters**. They show **☯** instead of stars. `/fuse` lists parents **from highest level** and adds filters plus name search. `/shop` and `/inventory` gained **Search**. Battle skill select now shows the catalog description.

## 2.4.2 — 15 August 2026

**Evil Melody** and **Holy Melody** **dropped to once per phase, from repeating on extra Press Turns**. Taunt does not retarget them (lowest HP%).

## 2.4.1 — 15 August 2026

HP and MP cap **rose to 9,999, from 999**. STR/MAG/VIT/AGI/LCK stayed at 999. Rag's macca **dropped to 5,000 / 10,000 / 20,000, from 25,000 / 120,000 / 300,000**. Suginami Tunnels now include the four Elements for gem farming.

## 2.4.0 — 15 August 2026

**Mitama** infusion (Nocturne): Element × Element, Rag's Jewelry (gems or expensive macca), +20% on two stats up to 2× base (cap 999). `/shop` gained **Sell category** and **Sell all**. Incense left the shop.

## 2.3.1 — 15 August 2026

Combat stat cap **dropped to 999, from 9999**. Per-hit damage stayed at 99,999.

## 2.3.0 — 15 August 2026

**Infernal Hall**: 9 Fiends, 1 vs party, Candelabra. Red Discord buttons only on Close / Decline / Flee.

## 2.2.x — 14 August 2026

`/inventory` replaces `/compendium`. Skill Cards and demons paginate. Shop no longer sells Skill Cards.

## 2.1.x — 14 August 2026

ClickHouse telemetry. Shop categories and quantity 1–99. Expedition farm loot.

## 2.0.0 — 13 August 2026

Expedition: Amala Labyrinth, 5 Kalpas.

## 1.4.x — 13 August 2026

Exploration (Suginami → Mirages). 100-demon cap. `/info` shows every stat.

## 1.3.x — 13 August 2026

File-based catalog. Item shop by Renown rank. Magatsuhi. Mail can attach items.

## 1.2.0 — 13 August 2026

Magatsuhi, items, Renown, `/profile`.

## 1.1.0 — 12 August 2026

XP catch-up, moon in combat, short Refs.

## 1.0.0 — 12 August 2026

First public release: `/start`, spawns, `/battle`, `/fuse`, `/mail`, `/shop`.
