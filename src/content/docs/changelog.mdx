Dated record of what shipped. The bot is at **2.4.0**.

## 2.4.0 — 15 August 2026

**Mitama** infusion (Nocturne): Element × Element, Rag's Jewelry (gems or expensive macca), +20% on two stats up to 2× base (cap 999). `/shop` gained **Sell category** and **Sell all**. Incense left the shop.

## 2.3.1 — 15 August 2026

Combat stat cap **dropped to 999, from 9999**. Per-hit damage stayed at 99,999.

## 2.3.0 — 15 August 2026

**Infernal Hall**: 9 Fiends, 1 vs party, Candelabra. Red Discord buttons only on Close / Decline / Flee.

## 2.2.x — 14 August 2026

`/inventory` replaces `/compendium`. Skill Cards and demons paginate. Shop no longer sells Skill Cards.

## 2.1.x — 14 August 2026

ClickHouse telemetry. Shop categories and quantity 1–99. Expedition farm loot.
