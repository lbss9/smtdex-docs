## 1.0.0 — 12 August 2026

First public version of the SMT V Discord bot.

### Play

- `/start` with Pixie, Slime, Onmoraki, Cait Sith, or Angel, plus 500 macca.
- Wild spawns about every three minutes: capture by name or battle. DLC species do not spawn.
- Fled spawns keep the image and show when the demon appeared and left, without the name.
- `/moon` for phase and spawn odds.
- Stars 1–5; new/full moon and weekends (Brasília) push rare rolls.

### Combat

- `/battle` UI: **Arena** (PvE), **Duelo** (PvP parties), **Confisco** (one demon; the loser loses it).
- Parties of up to four via `/party`.
- Battle XP follows SMT V’s `1.15ⁿ` level-difference curve. Duelo and Confisco do not pay XP or macca.

### Fusion, mail, shop

- `/fuse` with 2 / 3 / 4 parents and reverse. Result stars are the minimum of the parents.
- `/mail` holds demons in transit. **PayIn** lets the sender set a macca price the recipient must pay to receive the package.
- **Receber todos** claims free mail only.
- `/shop` daily skills. `/compendium` home with Demons and Skills. `/info`, `/balance`.

### Platform

- Postgres telemetry for battles, skills, fusions, spawns, and economy.
- Hourly database dumps on the host SSD with daily/weekly rotation so retention stays small.
- Player wiki in English and Portuguese.

Older command names (`/lua`, `/sacrifice`, `/trade`, `/collection`, `/skill`, `/battle pve`) are gone.
