Dated record of what shipped. The bot is at **3.0.0**.

## 3.0.0 — 18 August 2026

**Major rebalance — Nocturne alignment.** Full guide: [what's new in 3.0](../whats-new-300/).

- **7 star tiers** (was 5). 6★ 💠 Legendary, 7★ 🌈 Mythic.
- **Per-tier stat cap** (50 up to 5★ · 60 on 6★ · 75 on 7★). HP/MP still cap 9,999. Bosses bypass.
- **IVs removed.** Same species + tier = same base stats.
- **Mitama** now **+1 flat** per infusion (was +20% of current). Cap +20 total per stat.
- **Damage formula:** `((level + stat) × skill_power) ÷ 15`, Nocturne style.
- **`/inventory` and `/party` removed** — see [`/player`](../commands/#player).
- Datastore rebalanced (287 species re-tiered, 106 stat clamps). DB migration bumped every existing demon; **existing 5★ stay 5★** (no free promotion).

## 2.8.2 — 16 August 2026

The 21 Infernal Hall candelabra **are now key items**. They stay in Valuables and **cannot be sold**.

## 2.8.1 — 16 August 2026

Infernal Hall signatures **stay on the owner** and **do not inherit**. Everything else (active, passive, Magatsuhi) **inherits** and **drops as a Skill Card**. Arena/spawn card chance **rose to 50%, from 0%**; on a full moon **rose to 100%, from 50%**. See [Skill Cards](../skill-cards/).

## 2.8.0 — 16 August 2026

Infernal Hall **rose to 21 floors, from 9**. Wiki kits, fixed bosses.json, Morning Star summons the Morning Star.
