---
title: Magatsuhi
description: Party gauge 0–100 and Omagatoki skills.
---

A **0–100** gauge shared by the party, persistent between fights.

It fills in combat (end of turn, weakness/crit, damage taken, KO). Magatsuhi Shard/Crystal items also fill it.

When full, Magatsuhi skills (rank 50) appear in the skill select (cost `MAG`). Using one **drains the whole bar**, spends no HP/MP, and **does not consume a Press Turn** — the same demon acts again.

Omagatoki auras (Critical, Pierce, Charge, Hit, Dance, Free, Savage, Potential, Adversity, Doubler, Exploit, Momentum, Conserve, Shield of God) last until the start of that side’s next turn.
