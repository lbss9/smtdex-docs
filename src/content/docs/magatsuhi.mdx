A **0–100** **party** gauge that persists between fights. Shown on the battle embed and `/profile`.

## How it fills

| Event | Gain |
| --- | ---: |
| End of your party's turn | **+18** |
| Weakness or crit | **+8** |
| Damage taken | **+6** |
| KO dealt | **+10** |
| Magatsuhi Shard | **+40** |
| Magatsuhi Crystal | **100** (full) |

Shard: shop rank 3 (2,500 macca) or Haunt/Jaki unique **18%**. Crystal: **shop only**, rank 5 (20,000). Routes: [where to farm](../farm/).

## How to use in battle

1. The demon must have the Magatsuhi skill on its moveset (rank 50).
2. The gauge must be **full (100)**. Otherwise the skill **vanishes** from the select.
3. When full it appears with cost **MAG**.
4. Using it **empties the bar**, **costs no HP/MP**, and **does not spend a Press Turn** — the same demon acts again.

Skill Potential does **not** apply to Magatsuhi.

## How to get the skill

Buy the card at [`/rags`](../shop/#rags-jewelry--demons) → **Magatsuhi** tab → **Jade only** (3 / 4 / 6 / 8 / 12 by skill rank). Teach it to **any demon** in `/inventory`.

Fusion does **not** inherit Magatsuhi. Exploration and expedition **never** drop Magatsuhi cards.

Full list: [skill catalog](../catalog-skills/#magatsuhi-shop-list).

## Omagatoki

Party aura until the **start of the next turn of the same side**. Ward (Shield of God) lasts through the enemy turn.

| Skill | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Critical | Every hit crits (magic included) |
| Pierce | Pierces resist / null / repel / drain |
| Charge | Charge on physical / Concentrate on magic |
| Hit | Accuracy and ailment ×1.25 |
| Dance | Multi-hit uses the maximum hits |
| Free | Following skills cost no HP/MP |
| Savage | Damage ×2 and cost ×2 |
| Potential | Potential at maximum (+9) |
| Adversity | More damage at low HP (up to ×1.34) |
| Doubler | Kaja/Kunda ×2 |
| Exploit | Extra weakness damage (×1.21) |
| Momentum | +1 Press Turn icon on KO |
| Conserve | A normal action spends like a half (weak) |
| Shield of God | Incoming damage ×0.5 |

Not implemented in combat yet: **Luck**, **Bounty**, **Sincerity**, **Strategize**.

Offense (Big Bang, Freikugel EX, Soul Drain, Holy Wrath, Feline Fury…) uses the normal damage engine. Mapped support: Fairy Banquet (Luster), Rasetsu Feast (Debilitate), Dekajaon, Expand, Bouncy Body, Guardian Angels, Four Heavenly Edicts, Oni Formation, Eternal Prayer, Accursed Poison.
