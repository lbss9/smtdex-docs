`/fuse` opens a private UI. Favorites are not consumed. The parent list is **highest level first** and **omits Mitamas**. Use the filter (stars, Elements, race) or **Search** by nickname, species, or race.

## Modes

- **Fusion 2** — race chart, two-parent specials, **Element × Element → Mitama**.
- **Fusion 3 / 4** — named special recipes.
- **Reverse** — pick a result, then recipes you can actually make (Mitamas excluded).
- **Mitama Chamber** — pick the demon, Mitama type, and quantity.

Fusion 2 needs two demons. **Rag's Jewelry** is in `/shop`.

## Stars and odds

The result’s stars are the **minimum of the parents**. Infusion does **not** create a new species: the recipient stays.

| Tier | Success |
| --- | --- |
| Dyad (including Element → Mitama) | High |
| Special | Medium |
| Legendary | Low |
| DLC | Very low |
| Mitama infusion | **100%** |

A fail still spends macca and **keeps the parents**. An accident consumes them.

## Mitama Chamber

Mitamas **never spawn** and **cannot join a party or fight**. They show **☯**, not stars. Get them by fusing two different Elements or buying at Rag's, then infuse in the Chamber. Mitamas are **not** fusion parents in Fusion 2 / 3 / 4 or Reverse.

Each infusion raises **two stats by 20%** (floor +1) until **2× unbuffed base** (capped at 999). HP and MP are **not** raised by Mitama.

| Mitama | Stats |
| --- | --- |
| Ara | STR + AGI |
| Nigi | MAG + LUC |
| Kusi | VIT + AGI |
| Saki | VIT + LUC |

Skills only fill **empty slots** (max 8). If you have several of the same Mitama, **Quantity** infuses the stack in one go.
