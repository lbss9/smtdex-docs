title: Fusion

`/fuse` opens a private fusion UI.

## Modes

- **Fusion 2** — race chart or a two-parent special recipe.
- **Fusion 3** — special recipes that need three parents.
- **Fusion 4** — special recipes that need four parents.
- **Reverse** — pick a result, then see recipes you can actually make with the demons you own (dyad and special 2 / 3 / 4).

Favorites are not consumed. Confirm the preview before you pay.

## Stars

The result’s stars are the **minimum of the parents**. The preview names the parent that limited the stars.

## Cost and odds

Macca cost scales with the result’s level and tier:

| Tier | Typical targets | Success (approx.) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dyad | Regular race-chart results | High |
| Special | Named special recipes | Medium |
| Legendary | Masakado, Lucifer, Satan, Beelzebub, Shiva, Zeus, Michael, Samael | Low |
| DLC | Artemis, Cleopatra, Mephisto, Konohana Sakuya, Dagda, Demi-fiend, Demi-fiend A, Nuwa, Nuwa A | Very low |

A failed attempt still spends macca. An accident can produce a different demon in a nearby level band.

## DLC

DLC species **never spawn**. Fusion is the only way to obtain them, and the success rate is the lowest in the table.
