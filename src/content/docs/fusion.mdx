`/fuse` opens a private UI. Favorites are not consumed.

## Modes

- **Fusion 2** — race chart, two-parent specials, **Element × Element → Mitama**, or **Mitama infusion**.
- **Fusion 3 / 4** — named special recipes.
- **Reverse** — pick a result, then recipes you can actually make.
- **Rag's Jewelry** — buy Elements and Mitamas with **gems** or **macca** (macca is much more expensive). The premium Mitama **rotates** when you enter.

Fusion 2 needs two demons; Rag's still opens without them.

## Stars and odds

The result’s stars are the **minimum of the parents**. Infusion does **not** create a new species: the recipient stays.

| Tier | Success |
| --- | --- |
| Dyad (including Element → Mitama) | High |
| Special | Medium |
| Legendary | Low |
| DLC | Very low |
| Mitama infusion | **100%** |

A fail still spends macca and **keeps the parents**. An accident consumes them.

## Mitama infusion

Mitamas **never spawn**. Get them by fusing two different Elements, buying at Rag's, then infusing into the demon you want to buff.

Each infusion raises **two stats by 20%** (floor +1) until **2× unbuffed base** (capped at 999):

| Mitama | Stats |
| --- | --- |
| Ara | STR + AGI |
| Nigi | MAG + LUC |
| Kusi | VIT + AGI |
| Saki | VIT + LUC |

Skills only fill **empty slots** (max 8).

## Rag's Jewelry

| Offer | Gems | Macca |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Element | 1 Jade | 25,000 |
| Mitama | 1 Jade + 1 color gem | 120,000 |
| Premium | 3 Jade + 2 color | 300,000 |

Colors: Ara Ruby, Nigi Amethyst, Kusi Opal, Saki Sapphire. Farm guide: [Items & gems](../items/).
