Macca is the currency. Check `/balance`. You start with 500 from `/start`.

## Where macca comes from

Arena, fight spawns, exploration, successful expeditions, shop sales, mail. **Not** from chat.

You spend it on fusion, Rag's Jewelry (expensive), and `/shop`.

## XP

Only **Arena**, **fight spawns**, **exploration**, and successful **expeditions** grant XP. Duelo and Confisco do not.

The curve is SMT V’s: `level × 8 × 1.15ⁿ` per enemy, `n` clamped −10 to +10. Macca from those fights is `level × 12` per enemy (Arena/spawn). Exploration pays a fraction of that.

## Renown

It only goes up, to 1,000,000. The shop reads item rank (1–5; rank 5 at 2,500 Renown). `/profile` titles keep climbing after that.
