---
title: Macca & XP
description: How battle rewards work. Chat does not grant XP or macca.
---

Macca is the in-game currency. Check your balance with `/balance`. You start with 500 macca from `/start`.

## Where macca comes from

- Winning an **Arena** fight or a wild spawn battle.
- Receiving mail that includes macca.

Macca is spent on fusion attempts and the daily skill shop.

## Battle XP

Only **Arena** and **spawn battles** grant XP, and only if the player side wins. Duelo and Confisco do not pay XP or macca.

XP follows Shin Megami Tensei V’s level-difference curve: each enemy contributes `enemyLevel × 8 × 1.15ⁿ`, where `n` is the level gap versus that party member, clamped between −10 and +10. Higher-level enemies pay more; over-leveled members earn less.

Macca from those fights is `enemyLevel × 12` per enemy, summed.

Talking in chat does **not** grant XP or macca. Progression comes from battles, fusion, shop skills, and mail.
