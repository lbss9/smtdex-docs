## Demons

List, detail, delete. Cap **100** (pending mail does not count). An expedition locks delete and learn. Mitamas: **☯**, no stars, out of party. `/info` shows level, IVs, stars, and Mitama.

## Learning skills

1. **Skill Cards** tab → card → demon.
2. **Any demon** can learn any Magatsuhi.
3. The card is **consumed**.
4. Full moveset (**8**): pick the slot to replace.
5. A demon on an expedition cannot learn.
