`/inventory` replaces `/compendium`. Private UI: **Items** | **Demons** | **Close**.

## Items

| Tab | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| Common | Heal, revive, status, support, dampener, attack, Magatsuhi |
| Valuables | Sell loot (junk, gems, bars, Candelabra) |
| Talisman | Sealed |
| Skill Cards | Learn on a demon; paginated 25 at a time |

**Use** only on heal / revive / status / Magatsuhi. Charge, dampeners, and attack gems are **battle-only**.

## Demons

List, detail, delete. Cap **100** (pending mail does not count). An expedition locks delete and learn. `/info` shows effective stats (level, IVs, stars, Mitama), cap **999**.
