`/shop` opens **Buy**, **Sell**, **Rag's Jewelry**, and **Close**. The item catalog is everything unlocked by your Renown rank (not a daily roll). Rank 1 at 0; rank 2 at 50; rank 3 at 200; rank 4 at 800; rank 5 at 2,500.

**Skill Cards are not sold in the shop.** They come from expeditions, exploration, and mail.

## Buy

Pick a type (Heal, Revive, Support, …), an item, then **Buy** and type quantity 1–99. **Search** filters the list by name. Stack size caps the purchase.

## Sell

- **Sell** — quantity 1–99 of the selected item.
- **Sell category** — the entire stack of that category (no 99 cap).
- **Sell all** — every sellable item in the bag.

Category and All ask you to **confirm**. Gems and junk are meant to be sold; Rag's gems are worth more as Mitama fuel.

## Rag's Jewelry

Stock on entry: 4 Elements + 4 Mitamas + 1 premium (rotates). Pay with gems **or** macca. **Quantity** 1–99, capped by roster space and wallet.

| Offer | Gems | Macca |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Element | 1 Jade | 5,000 |
| Mitama | 1 Jade + 1 color gem | 10,000 |
| Premium | 3 Jade + 2 color | 20,000 |

Colors: Ara Ruby, Nigi Amethyst, Kusi Opal, Saki Sapphire.

The shop is private. Use **Close**, or let it idle for one minute.
