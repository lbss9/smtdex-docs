---
title: Shop
description: The daily skill shop and how purchases work.
---

`/shop` shows up to **ten** skills for the day. The offer list is the same for everyone and refreshes with the calendar day.

Buy with macca. Purchased skills go to your skill inventory; equip them on a demon from `/compendium`.

The shop UI is private. Use **Fechar**, or let it idle for one minute.
