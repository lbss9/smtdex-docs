`/shop` opens **Items**, **Sell**, and **Close**. The item catalog follows your **Renown rank** (not a daily roll). Offers live in the **menu**, not the embed body. **Rag's Jewelry** is [`/rags`](../shop/#rags-jewelry--demons).

| Shop rank | Renown |
| --- | ---: |
| 1 | 0 |
| 2 | 50 |
| 3 | 200 |
| 4 | 800 |
| 5 | 2,500 |

Normal Skill Cards are **not** sold in the item store — they come from Arena/spawn (**50%**, **100%** on a full moon), [exploration](../exploration/), [expedition](../expedition/), and mail. Actives and passives. Signatures **do not**. **Magatsuhi** is sold at `/rags` and can also drop. See [Skill Cards](../skill-cards/).

Full list: [item catalog](../catalog-items/).

## Buy

1. Pick a type: Heal, Revive, Status, Support, Dampeners, Attack, Magatsuhi (Shard/Crystal), Sell.
2. **Search** filters by name.
3. **Buy** asks for quantity **1–99**, capped by `maxStack` (Medicine max 50).
4. Price = `buy × quantity`.

Rank highlights: rank 1 Medicine/Life Stone; rank 3 **Magatsuhi Shard** (2,500) and Revival Bead; rank 5 Soma, Bead of Life, and **Magatsuhi Crystal** (20,000). Incense is **not** in the shop.

## Sell

- **Sell** — 1–99 of the selected item.
- **Sell category** — the whole stack in that category (no 99 cap).
- **Sell all** — the entire sellable bag.

Category and All ask **Confirm sale**. Rag's gems sell (250–400) but that is a **bad** deal if you still need Mitamas.

## Rag's Jewelry — Demons

`/rags` (no longer a `/shop` tab). Stock on enter: **4 Elements + 4 Mitamas + 1 premium** (premium **rolls**). Pay with gems **or** macca. **Mitama** up to **20** per buy; Element up to **99**. Limited by roster room (**100**) and wallet.

| Offer | Gems | Macca |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Element | 1 Jade | 5,000 |
| Mitama | 1 Jade + 1 color gem | 10,000 |
| Premium | 3 Jade + 2 color | 20,000 |

Colors: Ara **Ruby**, Nigi **Amethyst**, Kusi **Opal**, Saki **Sapphire**. Farm: [Suginami](../farm/).

## Rag's Jewelry — Magatsuhi

**Magatsuhi** tab. **Every** **active** Magatsuhi (54). Unique Miracle passives are **not** sold.

- **Jade only.** Macca **cannot** buy Magatsuhi.
- Shop rank **1–5** (skill power, not Renown): **3 / 4 / 6 / 8 / 12** Jade.
- Search and pages of 25.
- The card lands in Skill Cards. **Any demon** learns it in `/player`.

| Rank | Jade | Examples |
| ---: | ---: | --- |
| 1 | 3 | Omagatoki: Luck, light support |
| 2 | 4 | Hit, Dekajaon, low damage |
| 3 | 6 | Charge, Free, Fairy Banquet |
| 4 | 8 | Critical, Pierce, Savage, Eternal Prayer, Big Bang |
| 5 | 12 | Freikugel EX, Twilight Wave |

Full list: [skill catalog](../catalog-skills/#magatsuhi-shop-list).

The shop is private. **Close** or 1 idle minute ends it.
