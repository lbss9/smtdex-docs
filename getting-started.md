# Getting Started

## 1. Invite the bot

Ask a server administrator to invite SMTDex. The bot needs permission to **View
Channels**, **Send Messages**, **Embed Links**, and **Read Message History** in
the channels where you want demons to appear. It also uses the **Message Content**
intent so it can read the names players type when guessing a spawn.

## 2. Begin your journey

Run **`/start`** and choose a starter demon. This creates your player profile and
gives you your first demon.

```
/start demon: Jack Frost
```

## 3. Capture your first demon

Just chat. After enough messages in a channel, a wild demon appears with its
artwork. **Type its name** in the channel to capture it — first correct guess
wins.

## 4. Grow

- Keep chatting to earn **[Macca](macca.html)** and **[XP](leveling.html)**.
- Check your wallet with **`/balance`**.
- Browse your collection with **`/collection`**.
- Look up any demon with **`/info`** (start typing — names autocomplete).

That's it. Everything else — skills, fusion, trading, and battles — is covered in
the pages on the left.
